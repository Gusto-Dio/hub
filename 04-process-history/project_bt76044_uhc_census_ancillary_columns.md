**RESOLVED (2026-09-26, Oxford) — real root cause found, NOT Bri's ancillary-columns theory.** Bri's theory (remove carrier ancillary columns AF+ from the census before upload) was tested three ways and **disproven every time**: (1) blanking AF-BJ cell values, (2) fully deleting those columns, (3) adding the official UHC template's "Medical Coverage Type"/"Medical Waiver Reason" columns with valid values (`Waive`/`Medicaid`) mapped from Gusto's own `Waiver Reason - Medical` field. All three still hit the exact same "Employee Count Mismatch: Total does not match Total Eligible Employees" alert on the census page.

**Actual root cause:** `EnterCompanyInfo.xaml` already had code to work around this exact alert — it clicks "Enter Detailed Census" then "Plan Shopping" right after the census-red-alert guard block (~line 2373-2384), which is functionally the same manual workaround the user discovered independently ("click Enter Detailed Census before Plan Shopping resolves it"). But the coded click (`NClick_23`) had `InteractionMode="Simulate"` (its sibling "Plan Shopping" click does not) and there was **no delay** between the two clicks — the bot raced ahead of the portal's own recalculation of the Employee Count totals, reproducing the mismatch that a human's natural click-then-pause timing avoids.

**Fix applied (branch `BT-76044-oxford-census-ancillary-columns`):** in `EnterCompanyInfo.xaml`, removed `InteractionMode="Simulate"` from `NClick_23` ("Click 'Enter Detailed Census'") and added a 4-second `Delay` (`Delay_AfterEnterDetailedCensus`) between it and `NClick_10` ("Click 'Plan Shopping'"). Verified live in Staging: census now uploads and the flow proceeds cleanly from `/census` to `/plan-shopping` with **no** `Throw_11`/Employee Count Mismatch.
